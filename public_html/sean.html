The Person

SEÁN Ó'SÍOCHÁIN

Marketing Strategist & Copywriter | SEO, Brand Storytelling & Digital Campaigns | Music Producer & Audio Engineer | Creative Director

I'm a copywriter and marketing strategist who enjoys figuring out how brands communicate ideas in a way that actually connects with people.

My work mainly focuses on brand messaging, campaign strategy, and SEO-driven content. I'm interested in how companies build visibility online and strengthen their digital reputation through clear positioning, strong storytelling, and smart digital marketing.

Alongside marketing, I'm also a musician, producer, and audio engineer. I run MISE, a music project where I produce, engineer, and promote original tracks. Over the past 9 months, I've grown my following on Instagram from ~300 to 1,070 followers, leveraging content strategy, audience engagement, and social media marketing skills. I've also promoted and played 3 sold out shows so far in Anseo, SoundHouse and BackPage (IG: mise.gram).

Professionally, I'm interested in roles where strategy, communication, and client collaboration come together. That includes marketing strategy, account executive, and marketing executive positions where I can help develop campaigns, shape messaging, and support brand growth.

I'm also interested in topics around online presence optimization, professional networking, and career growth, particularly how people and brands build a strong presence and reputation online.

If you're working on something interesting in marketing, strategy, or creative projects, feel free to connect.
